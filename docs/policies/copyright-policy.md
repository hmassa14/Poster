# Copyright and attribution policy

1. **Link, do not copy.** Sources are summarised in the publication's own
   words and linked. The issue never reproduces an article, post, paper
   section, or documentation page.
2. **Quotation limits.** A direct quote is at most 40 words, and at most two
   quotes per source per issue. Quotes are verbatim, in quotation marks, and
   attributed by name and role with a link. `check_content` enforces the
   length limit; the critic enforces the per-source limit.
3. **Paywalled and members-only content** is cited by headline and
   publisher with a link, and described only from the portion the research
   stage could read or from the publisher's own summary.
4. **Images.** The system publishes no images it did not generate itself.
   If images are added later, they must be original, licensed, or public
   domain, with alt text from the SEO package.
5. **Code.** Code snippets from sources are not reproduced; the issue
   describes what the code does and links to it.
6. **Licences.** Open-weight model licences and dataset licences are
   reported by name with a link to the licence text.
7. **Takedown.** A rights holder's request is handled by the publisher
   within two business days: the passage is removed, the page updated with a
   note, and the case added to the eval suite.
